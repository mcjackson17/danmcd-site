A short bio

The career, in plain English.

I'm currently running Stick & Grow, an AI-enabled content studio I founded in 2011 and rebuilt around AI workflows in 2024.

Before this latest chapter, I was the CMO at Vouris for three years — a B2B sales consultancy where I owned brand, content, demand, and built a productized sales-copywriting training used by 50+ SaaS sales teams, including Hyros, Monograph, and RocketReach.

Before that, I was the Head Copy Coach at Neville Medhora's Kopywriting Kourse — co-hosting weekly Office Hours with Neville for two years, coaching more than 1,000 marketers and founders, and writing the program's cold sales email course.

And before all of that — UCL for a master's in public policy, Tufts for an undergrad in international relations, a stint at the United Nations, and a couple of years in cleantech and LED consulting that taught me how to write things that get funded.
