A few questions I get

Common questions.

Will you do hourly consulting?

Almost never. The sprint exists because hourly engagements make both of us worse at our jobs. If you genuinely need a one-time conversation, email me and we'll figure it out.

Do you take retainer clients?

Rarely, and only for engagements where there's a real system to build. I'm not your fractional CMO.

Who is the sprint really for?

Senior independent consultants, executive coaches, and experts who already have clients, already have revenue, and are trying to graduate from "I do projects" to "I sell a thing." Usually $150K–$700K in annual revenue, working solo or with a couple of freelancers.

How do I know if I'm a fit?

Read the letter for a few weeks. If the essays feel like they're describing your business, we're probably a fit. If they don't, we're definitely not.
